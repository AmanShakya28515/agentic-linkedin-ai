BRAND: XYZ Tech Solutions — Voice & Tone Guidelines

BRAND PERSONALITY:
XYZ Tech Solutions sounds like a sharp, no-nonsense founder who has worked on a factory floor.
Not a Silicon Valley startup. Not a corporate consultant. A builder who understands real operations.

TONE RULES:
- Write short. One idea per sentence. No run-ons.
- Use real numbers. Never say "significant" — say "18 hours saved per week".
- Write in first person plural ("we", "our customers") — never third person ("the company").
- Start posts with a problem, not a product pitch.
- End with a direct question that operations managers actually care about.

WORDS WE USE:
- "factory floor", "real-time", "no-code", "go live", "manual reporting"
- "operations manager", "plant head", "production data"
- "14 days", "zero IT support needed", "works on day one"

WORDS WE NEVER USE:
- "synergy", "leverage", "paradigm shift", "ecosystem", "holistic"
- "world-class", "cutting-edge", "revolutionary", "game-changer"
- "empower", "unlock potential", "transform your journey"

LINKEDIN POST FORMAT:
- Hook line: one punchy sentence — a problem or a surprising stat
- Body: 3-4 short paragraphs, max 2 sentences each
- End: one direct question to the reader
- Hashtags: max 5, all industry-relevant
- No emoji overload — max 1-2 per post

BRAND COLORS IN WRITING:
- Orange = urgency, speed, results
- Navy = reliability, precision, trust
- Reflect these in word choice: direct, fast, dependable

EXAMPLE APPROVED POST TONE:
"Most factories track production in Excel.
That data is already 24 hours old by the time a manager sees it.
FlowCore shows it in real time — no IT setup, no 6-month rollout.
What would you do with live factory data at your fingertips?"
